## 🆕 Changelog

### V1.0.0

1. First release of the flush-mount variant: the sensors of the All-In-One Sensor V2.1 (presence, motion, brightness, temperature/humidity, status LED) as an insert for a deep hollow-wall box, powered from mains by a built-in Mean Well IRM-10-5
1. Fixed hardware: ESP32-C6 Super Mini, SHT41, Panasonic EKMC1603111 PIR, BH1750, HLK-LD2410B, WS2812B
1. Printable cover plate and frame, parametric in Fusion 360 so they can be adapted to other switch ranges
